The 3rd Fitness Lab

The 3rd Fitness Lab is a one-stop fitness center in Quezon City furnished with advanced equipment and machines, an artificial turf, and a full-sized basketball court (that can be converted into a volleyball, badminton or pickleball court), this sports hub is a haven for elite athletes and leagues.

Aside from being a gym and physical center, there are coaches who can instruct specific training programs for strengthening & conditioning exercises or bulking up properly, as well as proper nutrition for those who want to lose weight the right & healthy way.
