The 3rd Fitness Lab

The 3rd Fitness Lab is a one-stop fitness center in Quezon City furnished with advanced equipment and machines, an artificial turf, and a full-sized basketball court (that can be converted into a volleyball, badminton or pickleball court), this sports hub is a haven for elite athletes and leagues.

Aside from being a gym and physical center, there are coaches who can instruct specific training programs for strengthening & conditioning exercises or bulking up properly, as well as proper nutrition for those who want to lose weight the right & healthy way.

Classes

  • Zumba

    Zumba sessions at The 3rd Fitness Lab are high-energy, vibrant, and accessible for all fitness levels, ensuring no one feels left behind. Regulars often highlight the welcoming vibe of the studio, where the focus is on movement and enjoyment rather than perfect technique. Also, the sessions are done in a well-maintained space with good acoustics, which enhances the overall group exercise experience.

  • Yoga

    Yoga sessions at The 3rd Fitness Lab are designed to complement their high-intensity offerings, providing a space for members to focus on recovery, flexibility, and mindfulness. These sessions are part of a holistic approach to fitness that balances strength training and cardio with restorative movement.

  • HIIT

    (High Intensity Interval Trainings)

    Sessions utilize a wide range of functional tools, including TRX suspension trainers, rowers, dumbbells, kettlebells, battle ropes, and spin bikes to keep the workouts varied and challenging. Unlike a standard group gym, coaches at the lab emphasize proper technique and form throughout the high-speed intervals to ensure safety and effectiveness.
